16th Quadrennial General Conference

The Association of African Universities (AAU) announces its 16th Quadrennial General Conference under the theme: Shaping the Future of Higher Education for Innovation and Sustainable Development of Africa.

This conference aims to address critical challenges in African higher education, promote innovative approaches, and foster collaboration among institutions across the continent.

The Conference will further serve as a stakeholders’ platform for engagement with the higher education community in Africa. Between 500 – 700 delegates are expected, comprising AAU member universities, higher education policymakers, development partners, researchers, students, and other education stakeholders.

Where

University Mohammed VI Polytechnic (UM6P), Rabat, Morocco

When

July 21-25, 2025

Call for Submissions

We invite researchers, policymakers, educators, and practitioners to contribute to the transformation of higher education in Africa by submitting their abstracts, papers, and case studies.

Your submissions should align with the conference sub-themes and provide innovative insights, practical solutions, and actionable recommendations for higher education, innovation, and sustainable development.

Submission Guidelines

  • ✓ Submissions must clearly state the narrative type, thematic focus, and relevance.
  • ✓ Include brief bio of presenter(s) and contact information.
  • ✓ Abstract length: 150-300 words.
  • ✓ Full paper limit: 6,000 words.
  • ✓ Submissions accepted in English or French.

Call for Expression of Interest to Organise Special Sessions

The Organizing Committee of the 16th Quadrennial General Conference of the Association of African Universities (AAU)
is pleased to invite institutions, groups, networks, projects, and various categories of organisations
to express interest in organising special sessions at the General Conference of the AAU

Call for Submissions

The General Conference, convened quadrennially, is the Association’s premier gathering, which brings together a diverse range of higher education stakeholders, including representatives of AAU member institutions, associate members, and observers.

This excellent opportunity to organize special sessions will enable institutions to bring together their stakeholders and experts to discuss specific topics, showcase innovative projects, and facilitate networking.

Submission Guidelines

  • ✓ Session title and brief description
  • ✓ Session format and duration
  • ✓ Key points or topics to be addressed at the session
  • ✓ Relevance of session to Africa’s higher education community
  • ✓ Names and affiliations of session organizers and speakers
  • ✓ Expected outcomes and takeaways
