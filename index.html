16th Quadrennial General Conference (QGC)

The Association of African Universities (AAU) is proud to present the 16th Quadrennial General Conference with the theme: Shaping the Future of Higher Education for Innovation and Sustainable Development of Africa.

This conference aims to address critical challenges in African higher education, promote innovative approaches, and foster collaboration among institutions across the continent.

Where

University Mohammed VI Polytechnic (UM6P), Rabat, Morocco

When

July 21-25, 2025
