# 16th Quadrennial General Conference (QGC) of the Association of African Universities (AAU)

Location: Rabat, Morocco
Date: July 7-11, 2025

## Theme:
Shaping the Future of Higher Education for Innovation and Sustainable Development of Africa

The Association of African Universities (AAU) invites higher education leaders, policymakers,
researchers, development partners, and industry stakeholders to the 16th Quadrennial General Conference (QGC).
This landmark event will explore innovative solutions to transform African Higher Education Institutions (HEIs),
 address Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), and empower future generations.

## Key Sub-Themes:
1. Policy and Advocacy for Transforming Higher Education
2. AI and Technology-Driven Teaching and Learning in Higher Education Institutions
3. Curriculum Re-engineering and Credit Transfer Systems
4. Indigenous Knowledge Systems and Environmental Sustainability
5. Funding and Infrastructure Development for Higher Education
6. Higher Education's Contribution to the SDGs
7. Mainstreaming E-learning and Addressing the Digital Divide
8. Engaging the African Diaspora in Higher Education Development
9. Promoting Institutional Collaboration and Partnerships

## Objectives:
* Advocate for transformative policies in African higher education.
* Harness AI and digital technologies to enhance teaching and learning.
* Develop innovative curricula and harmonized credit transfer systems.
* Incorporate indigenous knowledge for sustainable environmental solutions.
* Explore sustainable funding models and infrastructure investments.
* Assess HEIs' role in achieving SDGs across Africa.
* Bridge the digital divide and advance e-learning solutions.
* Strengthen African diaspora engagement in knowledge exchange.
* Foster inter-institutional partnerships to enhance higher education collaboration.

## Why Attend?
* Gain insights from higher education leaders and policymakers.
* Contribute to key policy discussions shaping African HEIs.
* Explore innovative funding, technology, and curriculum strategies.
* Expand your professional network with institutions and partners.
* Be part of Africa's higher education transformation.
